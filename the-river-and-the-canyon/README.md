# The River and the Canyon — files in this folder

Three pieces live here, each in three formats. **Markdown (`.md`)** renders right in your browser — easiest to read on GitHub. **PDF** is the typeset version for reading offline or printing. **Word (`.docx`)** is there if you want to edit or annotate.

**Start here:**

- **`the-river-and-the-canyon-lean`** — the lean edition. The whole picture, tokenization through generation, in one fast pass, then where the analogy breaks. Read this first.
- **`the-river-and-the-canyon`** — the full paper. Same arc, but it develops each step in depth and stress-tests every claim against the actual mechanism.
- **`no-mountain-in-the-sentence`** — a short companion essay on the *method*: the discipline for trusting an analogy only as far as it earns.

`assets/` holds the figures the full paper references.

The companion paper, *What Kind of Water Carves the Mountain?*, is in [`../what-kind-of-water/`](../what-kind-of-water/). Notes and proposals are in [`../notes/`](../notes/). See the [top-level README](../README.md) for the whole project.
