River & Canyon · v1.0 · the claim boundary

What this method
can and cannot decide

A behavioral stress-retention probe. Each limit names the method that would be required instead.

Can decide — behaviorally
Whether retention differs
narrow vs matched broad, as a measured ΔR with a confidence interval
Whether a stable answer is wrong
retention + counterexample + error-identity, read jointly
Whether a failure is the handoff
forced-intermediate rescue, causally — vs subskill weakness
Whether a signal is an artifact
cross-calibration invariance + state-load-matched controls
That retention ≠ correctness
analytic — true by the metric's own definition
Cannot decide — needs other method
Which circuit causes it
needs activation patching / attribution
Whether "defects" literally exist
a claim about internal weights — no behavioral access
Why a behavior fails internally
needs ablation / salience / probing
Whether it generalizes
one model is a pilot — needs replication
Whether it predicts deployment
the open question — needs the run, then field data
"But how do you know it's not circuit X?"
We don't, and we don't claim to — that lives in the right column. This is a metrology instrument, not a mechanism finder.

Rule: if it requires circuits, it is not this project.
