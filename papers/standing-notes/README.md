# Standing Notes

This sub-tree holds **paper-class governance artifacts that are explicitly NOT released as numbered or lettered papers**. They have paper-like structure (versioned, often with PDF + figures), but the program has deliberately designated them as **standing governance notes** — methodological discipline rather than publications.

A standing note here:
- has its own version (e.g., v0.7.2) and may have a PDF + figures
- carries the same review discipline as a paper
- is *not* part of either paper lineage (the numbered metrology series `paper1/2/3-`, or the lettered instrument/stress dyad `paper-a-` / future `paper-b-`)
- lives here (not at `papers/paperN-` or `papers/paper-X-`) precisely because the program decided NOT to promote it to a paper

The sub-tree exists so paper-class standing artifacts are discoverable from `papers/` without erasing the "not a numbered/lettered paper" designation that placed them in this category in the first place.
