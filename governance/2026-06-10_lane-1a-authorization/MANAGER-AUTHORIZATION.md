## Verbatim memo

> To: Senior Engineer, CS Engineer
> Cc: Team Lead, Contributors
> From: Elias / Manager
> Re: Authorization to open Lane 1a under tightened negative-use doctrine
> Status: Lane 1a authorized for design and execution under stated
> constraints only
>
> Manager authorizes Lane 1a to open.
>
> This authorization is narrow.
>
> Lane 1a is authorized only as a pre-candidate, negative-use,
> certification-window occupancy / failure-map sweep.
>
> It is not candidate selection.
>
> It is not threshold-setting.
>
> It is not certification rehearsal.
>
> It is not certification evidence.
>
> It is not a stress-retention run.
>
> ## 1. Required ordering
>
> The sequence is now:
>
> ```text
> 1. Paper 3 v1.1 released and confirmed — COMPLETE
> 2. Lane 1a — AUTHORIZED BY THIS MEMO
> 3. Candidate Selection Memo — NOT AUTHORIZED
> 4. Threshold-sheet population / lock — NOT AUTHORIZED
> 5. Certification evaluation — NOT AUTHORIZED
> 6. Possible stress-retention run — NOT AUTHORIZED
> ```
>
> Paper 3 v1.1 is the operative framework.
>
> The default lock-eligible framework identifier is:
>
> ```text
> paper3-certification-protocol-v1.1
> ```
>
> ## 2. Lane 1a doctrine
>
> Lane 1a operates under the following doctrine:
>
> ```text
> Lane 1a may rule out.
> Lane 1a may not rule in.
> ```
>
> More precisely:
>
> ```text
> Lane 1a may eliminate clearly non-viable regions.
> Non-eliminated regions remain an unordered survivor set.
> Lane 1a may not rank, recommend, shortlist, positively favor, or
>     select any construction.
> Lane 1a may not shape thresholds.
> Lane 1a may not reduce later candidate-selection rigor.
> Lane 1a may not become certification evidence.
> ```
>
> Survivorship is information, but it is not authorization, ranking, or
> positive evidence.
>
> ## 3. Authorized work
>
> The following work is authorized:
>
> ```text
> 1. Senior prepares a Lane 1a design packet.
> 2. CS prepares the Lane 1a execution packet and locked output schema.
> 3. Team Lead reviews the packet under the adversarial failure-mode
>    review standard.
> 4. Manager reviews the final execution packet before first data access.
> 5. If the packet passes, CS may execute the pre-registered Lane 1a sweep.
> ```
>
> This memo authorizes opening the Lane 1a lane and preparing the
> execution packet.
>
> It does not authorize first data access until the final packet is
> reviewed and Manager confirms the execution start.
>
> ## 4. Required pre-registration before any data access
>
> Before Lane 1a can run, the packet must pre-register:
>
> ```text
> model
> task family
> ladder / variants
> N
> diagnostic axes
> classification criteria
> output schema
> plotting rules
> fixed outcome language
> exclusion language
> B1 v2 provenance capture plan
> regression / winner's-curse warning
> consumption-side restrictions
> ```
>
> The analysis script, plotting style, output schema, and classification
> criteria must be locked and hash-recorded before first data access.
>
> ## 5. Output form restrictions
>
> Lane 1a outputs must use descriptive, pre-registered labels only.
>
> Allowed label style:
>
> ```text
> accuracy_indistinguishable_from_token_prior
> accuracy_indistinguishable_from_declared_policy_envelope
> insufficient_measurement_headroom
> strict_content_gap_instability
> abstention_contract_instability
> requires_further_investigation
> inconclusive_not_actionable
> ```
>
> Do not use gate-verdict-shaped labels such as:
>
> ```text
> fails_D1
> fails_D2
> certification_ready
> near_certifiable
> promising
> viable
> preferred
> recommended
> ```
>
> The label `requires_further_investigation` is neutral. It means only
> "not ruled out under this sweep."
>
> ## 6. Visualization restrictions
>
> Lane 1a must not create positive visual artifacts.
>
> Prohibited:
>
> ```text
> heat maps
> contours
> smoothed curves
> fitted boundaries
> threshold lines
> certification bands
> viability overlays
> promising-region annotations
> ranked cluster plots
> ```
>
> Allowed:
>
> ```text
> unannotated per-rung diagnostic points
> discrete categorical failure / uncertainty markers
> auditable tables of pre-registered diagnostic values
> ```
>
> Operating rule:
>
> ```text
> Show data.
> Do not draw a road to a winner.
> ```
>
> ## 7. Artifact tags
>
> Lane 1a artifacts must carry:
>
> ```text
> artifact_class: lane-1a-reconnaissance
> certification_relevance: none
> ```
>
> Lane 1a outputs are excluded from threshold design.
>
> They are excluded from certification evidence.
>
> They are excluded from D6 historical-information allowance for
> threshold derivation.
>
> ## 8. Consumption-side rule
>
> A later Candidate Selection Memo may cite Lane 1a only for coarse
> elimination.
>
> It may not use Lane 1a to rank, prefer, shortlist, or positively
> justify a candidate.
>
> A later threshold sheet may not copy, transform, tune from, or
> reference Lane 1a statistics.
>
> Required attestation for any later threshold-sheet process:
>
> ```text
> No statistic computed in Lane 1a was copied into any threshold-sheet
> field, directly or by transformation.
> ```
>
> ## 9. Winner's-curse / regression warning
>
> Lane 1a survivors are expected to perform worse during fresh
> certification than during sweep exploration.
>
> Regression from Lane 1a behavior during later certification is not
> instrument failure.
>
> It must not be used to tune thresholds after the fact.
>
> ## 10. Required failure-mode review
>
> The Lane 1a packet must answer the standing review question:
>
> ```text
> Assume this proposal is approved, implemented by disciplined people,
> and still causes a downstream claim or governance failure. Describe
> the path by which that happens. Then state what must be changed so
> the failure is structurally blocked rather than merely forbidden.
> ```
>
> At minimum, reviewers must answer:
>
> ```text
> How could reconnaissance become pre-selection?
> How could a diagnostic artifact become positive evidence?
> How could unordered survivors become an implicit ranking?
> How could visual layout create a "good region"?
> How could Lane 1a data contaminate threshold design?
> How could a descriptive label become a gate verdict?
> ```
>
> Null findings require shown search. A bare "no issues found" is not
> sufficient.
>
> ## 11. Required CS posture
>
> CS may prepare the Lane 1a packet and verification structure.
>
> CS may not begin first data access until Manager confirms the final
> execution packet.
>
> CS must preserve:
>
> ```text
> B1 v2 provenance capture
> locked schema
> locked script hash
> locked plotting rules
> locked classification criteria
> artifact hashes
> audit log
> ```
>
> B1 v2.1 is not authorized.
>
> ## 12. Non-authorizations
>
> This authorization does not authorize:
>
> ```text
> candidate selection
> candidate ranking
> candidate shortlist
> threshold-sheet population
> threshold lock
> certification evaluation
> INT8 / INT4 stress-retention run
> B1 v2.1 implementation
> Claim C activation
> Fork A reactivation
> Paper 3 application beyond Lane 1a packet use
> Paper 6 activation
> public benchmark packaging
> ```
>
> All work beyond Lane 1a packet preparation and Manager-approved
> execution remains closed.
>
> ## 13. Manager decision
>
> Lane 1a is authorized to open under the constraints above.
>
> Senior and CS may prepare the Lane 1a packet.
>
> First data access requires final packet review and Manager
> confirmation.
>
> — Elias / Manager
