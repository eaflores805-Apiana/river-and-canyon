## 6. Process lessons (the durable part)

```text
LESSON 1 — location ≠ channel, recurring. Three times this phase, an artifact was
  "filed" in one seat's view but unreadable from another (synthesis draft;
  constructed inputs; validation result bytes; and Senior's own closeout v0.2).
  Standing rule, now adopted by both Senior and CS: a result is FILED only when
  its bytes verify from the shared remote on a clean fetch — not when a commit
  SHA is reported. CS has added a post-push remote-HEAD verification block; Senior
  bundles-or-pushes built artifacts in the same motion as the return.
LESSON 2 — gate discipline survives a good result. The INT8 rung produced a
  scientifically clean pattern, AND it was procedurally nonconforming, and the
  project correctly quarantined it anyway. A useful result obtained off-sequence
  does not retroactively become an official milestone. This is the program's
  fail-closed logic applied to its own process, not just its science.
LESSON 3 — framing narrows under authority. Senior's interpretation used
  "RETENTION-PASS as scoped"; the Manager's classification forbids "retention
  established." The byte facts are unchanged; the framing is narrowed to the
  permitted wording. Records carry the Manager's framing forward, not the
  superseded scoped phrase.
LESSON 4 — map drift is caught by asking "where is the map." The route/map
  tension surfaced from a direct question and is now an explicit reconciliation
  item rather than a silent drift. The map of record predates the 1a′ detour;
  reconciling it is the gating next step before any successor rung.
LESSON 5 — verify the field, not the summary. Senior's first flip-count read the
  wrong output field (output_text vs raw_output) and found zero flips; recomputing
  against the correct field confirmed the two reported flips. Recompute from the
  actual bytes and the actual field, not from a plausible reconstruction.
```
