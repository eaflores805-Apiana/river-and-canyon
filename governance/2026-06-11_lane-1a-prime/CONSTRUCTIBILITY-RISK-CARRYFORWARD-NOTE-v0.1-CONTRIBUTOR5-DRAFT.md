## 2. NOT_RULED_OUT, decoded — the central non-inference

D4-A's accepted outcome is **NOT_RULED_OUT**: under the active five-criterion set, the instrument **attached no elimination label**. This is the *absence of an elimination*, not the *presence of a certification*.

```text
NOT_RULED_OUT means:  the declared elimination criteria did not fire on L01 here.
NOT_RULED_OUT does NOT mean:  certified · viable · clean · not-shortcut-driven ·
                              capable · ruled-in · ready · a candidate.
```

The drift to watch, stated so it can be caught: *not ruled out → not eliminated → stands → viable → certified.* Each step looks small; the chain is an over-claim. The instrument may rule out; **it may not rule in.** No formalized certification gate has yet been exercised — D4-A exercised an elimination/discrimination instrument, not a certification gate, and that sentence must remain true wherever this work is described.

## 4. Three interpretations of any future non-certification (held symmetrically, pre-data)

If a future authorized run does **not** yield a certifiable candidate, that outcome is consistent with at least three distinct worlds, and this note pre-commits to treating them as **a first-class open set**, not as a ranked guess:

```text
1. threshold miscalibration — the bar was set wrong for this construction/model/task.
2. gate-design defect — the gate's structure fails to admit a genuine candidate it should.
3. genuine constructibility barrier at this model / task / scale — no certifiable
   candidate exists to be admitted here, regardless of threshold or gate.
```

**Interpretation 3 is a first-class outcome, not a failure of the program.** A genuine constructibility barrier is a *result of record* — "a blocked gate is a result, not a delay." The note does **not** predict which of the three obtains; pre-registering an expected interpretation would be its own post-hoc framing. All three stay live until data distinguishes them, and the distinguishing evidence does not yet exist.

Symmetric guard (so this does not become premature pessimism): just as NOT_RULED_OUT is not certification, a future non-certification is not, by itself, proof of interpretation 3. Non-certification is gap-shaped — it fits all three worlds until a control separates them.

## 5. The forward hinge stays open and negative-use

Two questions remain genuinely unanswered and must not be quietly closed by momentum:

```text
(a) constructibility — does the task family contain certifiable candidates at this
    model/task/scale? UNKNOWN.
(b) predictive value — does certification under this instrument predict retention under
    stress / deployment reliability? UNKNOWN, and not yet testable by anything run.
```

Question (b) is the program's highest-value and highest-over-read forward claim — the "retention predicts reliability" hinge. It is recorded here only to keep it **open and negative-use**: this note may not be cited as progress toward it. The instrument measures (when authorized) retention cleanly; that a clean ruler exists is not evidence that what it measures matters. "We have improved the ruler; we have not yet mapped the territory."

## 6. Misuse paths this note itself creates, and their containment (foresight value-add)

A boundary note can become the very artifact it warns against. The paths:

```text
P1  Excerpt risk — the §1 positive clause ("validated, sealed, survived first contact")
    is lifted without "we do not yet know."
    Containment: §1 binds the clauses as one unit; capability guard stated inline.

P2  Laundering-into-selection — this note is later cited to "establish" the task family
    is hard (interp. 3), and that finding feeds candidate selection or sweep design.
    Containment: NEGATIVE-USE / NON-CONSUMPTION tag, §7. This note produces no input to
    candidate selection, ranking, threshold population, or sweep design.

P3  Funder/outside framing — "we identified a constructibility barrier" or "a tool that
    measures whether quantization breaks composition" is read out of this note.
    Containment: §3 + §5 non-claims; the SBIR/outside-facing door is a separate
    Manager-gated decision and this note is not evidence for it.

P4  Pessimism inversion — interp. 3 is treated as the established reading, retiring the
    construction prematurely.

```
