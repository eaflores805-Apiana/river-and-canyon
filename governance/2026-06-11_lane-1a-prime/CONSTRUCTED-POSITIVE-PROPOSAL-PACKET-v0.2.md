## 3. Do P1/P2/P3 close all desk prerequisites from v0.1?

**Yes.** v0.1 §8 named exactly three prerequisites and they map one-to-one onto the cleared artifacts (P2↔defect, P1↔calibration, P3↔manifest). No other desk prerequisite was named in v0.1, and none has arisen from the three clearances — each artifact's own shown-read confirmed surplus ABSENT and the §6 cross-checks in P3 confirmed mutual consistency. The desk layer is closed.

What is **not** closed, and was never a desk prerequisite, is the empirical layer: whether a calibrated clean member *realizes* off-ceiling against a real model, and whether the pair *realizes* a discriminating verdict. Those are gated (§6) and are precisely the content a construction decision would authorize testing — they are not blockers to *routing the proposal*, because the proposal asks for that decision, not for its answer.

## 4. Current proposal disposition

```text
DISPOSITION: PROPOSAL-READY
```

PROPOSAL-READY in the defined sense: the design is coherent (unchanged from v0.1), and the desk prerequisites that held it at CONDITIONAL are cleared and CS-verified. It can route to Manager for a separate construction/generation authorization decision. This is exactly the threshold v0.1 §8 pre-registered, reached by the means v0.1 specified — no scope was widened to get here.

PROPOSAL-READY does **not** mean the design may be built, and does **not** mean anything may be executed against a model. It means only that the proposal is ready to be *decided on*. The distinction is the whole discipline: the desk has carried this as far as paper can, and the next move is a Manager decision, not an execution.
