## 4. One scope caution to record at acceptance

C6 §6 correctly observes that Lane 1a's mis-specified control is, under honest naming, a *valid
different control*: `scrambled_binding_retrieval` measures whether a model follows new bindings after
rebinding. That taxonomy belongs in the addendum. It may **not** be used to retroactively reinterpret
the Lane 1a control numbers as a positive rebinding finding — the close-out's P4 citation scope and
the negative-use boundary govern all Lane 1a data, and a renamed construct does not re-open a closed
sweep's numbers for positive use. The addendum should state this in one sentence where the taxonomy is
introduced.
